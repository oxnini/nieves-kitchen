Every recipe in nieves-kitchen is halal. Never suggest or include alcohol (wine, beer, spirits, deglazing with wine, etc.) or other non-halal ingredients (pork, non-halal gelatin, etc.) in recipes, critiques, or chef suggestions.

**Why:** The cook stated all her recipes are halal and explicitly asked to never suggest wine again after a lasagna critique proposed a red-wine deglaze.

**How to apply:** When suggesting professional/chef improvements, screen out alcohol and non-halal items before presenting. Offer halal-compatible alternatives (e.g. stock, a splash of vinegar, pomegranate molasses) instead of wine. Relates to [[feedback_per_recipe_workflow]] if present.
