The draft was the driest of the three (2.3); it would bake drier and denser than the sources that agree on ~2.6. Moving to 210g flour, 170g yogurt (one standard UK pot) and 100ml juice brings it to 2.5, with the syrup adding moisture on top. Sugar kept at the draft's 180g (below both sources' 200g) because the loaf also carries 40g syrup sugar and 120g glaze. Baking powder 2 tsp per 210g flour matches Ina's 2 tsp per 190g; I left out bicarb for simplicity and no soapy-taste risk.
Batter ~970g in a 900g (2lb) tin: a little over half full, same load as the Mediterranean Dish 8.5 x 4.5in pan.

## What makes it not bog-standard
1. **Orange three ways, with the zest of three oranges** (Mediterranean Dish uses 3 oranges' zest; the draft had 2). Orange juice is mild and sweet, so the flavour has to come from peel oils; zest is rubbed into sugar until damp orange sand.
2. **Simmered syrup** (Borrowed Bites heats until clear; I add 2 minutes of gentle bubbling): concentrates the juice so the soak tastes of orange, not just sugar. A distinctly different move from the lemon loaf, which only warms its syrup.
3. **Oil streamed in while whisking after the eggs and yogurt** (emulsion; Mediterranean Dish whisks wet together), eggs whisked 1 minute with the zest sugar first for lift.
4. **Paper sling + 30-minute soak in the tin before lifting out** (Borrowed Bites cools fully in the tin; Savvy Bites soaks 2 hours): lets the syrup spread without the soft loaf breaking when moved.
5. **Thick glaze tested by the ribbon** with a spare spoonful of icing sugar listed as a rescue, glaze only on a loaf cold to the base.

## Verification
Ratio re-checked against two strong sources, fetched in full: The Mediterranean Dish Orange Cake (210g flour, 200g sugar, 170g Greek yogurt, 120ml olive oil, 3 large eggs, 120ml orange juice, 1.5 tsp baking powder + 1/2 tsp bicarb, zest of 3 oranges = 2 tbsp; glaze 125g icing sugar + 1.5 tbsp juice; 8.5 x 4.5in pan) and Barefoot Contessa Lemon Yogurt Cake (190g flour, 200g sugar in batter, 245g whole-milk yogurt, 120ml oil, 3 extra-large eggs, 2 tsp baking powder; syrup on warm cake after 10 min in the pan, glaze on cold). This loaf (210 flour, 180 sugar, 3 eggs, 120ml oil, 170g Greek yogurt + 100ml juice, 2 tsp baking powder) sits between them: same flour, oil and eggs, water from yogurt + juice ~216g vs ~208g (Contessa), sugar slightly lower, which suits a loaf that also carries syrup and glaze. 2 tsp baking powder alone is fine in an acidic batter (Contessa does the same). Glaze 120g : 1.5 tbsp is the Mediterranean Dish ratio. Bake 170°C (150°C fan), middle shelf, 50 to 60 min, foil tent at 35 min, crack/spring-back/skewer/95 to 98°C, paper sling: all sound. Nutrition re-worked at ~366 kcal/slice: 365 stands. Image re-checked (200, close match: glazed loaf with drips and orange slices, no faces; poppy-seed bowl only).

1. Room-temperature step: replaced a shaky claim (cold eggs "thicken the oil") with the true reason: a cold batter sets at the edges before the middle heats, giving a peak over a damp seam.
2. Zest: the Mediterranean Dish gets 2 tbsp from 3 oranges, so "about 3 tbsp" was optimistic; list now 2 tbsp ("all of it, less 1 tsp"), step says 2 to 3 tbsp, all of the rest goes in.
3. Syrup simmer kept (it is right for orange, as the lemon verifier noted) but the reason sharpened: thin, mild juice reduced slightly clings and sinks in slowly; capped at 2 minutes because a long boil tastes of marmalade, not fresh orange.
4. New tip: taste the juice; if the oranges are very sweet, add 1 tbsp lemon juice to the syrup so the soak stays bright against the sweet glaze (balance; three sweet orange layers can cloy with sweet navels). 7 tips now.
5. Times: active 35 → 40, total 270 → 300, resting 180 → 205 (re-walked: prep 15, batter 10, bake 55, tin 10, soak 5, tin 30, cool 120, glaze 5, set 45 = 295; the hour of warming eggs/yogurt sits outside, as in the lemon loaf). Headnote now says about 5 hours plus the hour beforehand.
6. Headnote: glaze sets "thick and opaque", not "white" (orange juice tints it ivory).
7. Almond variation: "start testing at 50 minutes" (same as the base) → "may need 5 minutes more", since ground almonds slow the bake.

Distinct from the lemon loaf: light olive oil, zest-led flavour with a simmered syrup, a thick drip glaze, the whole-blended-orange variation and its own copy and variations. No shared step wording beyond the tin-lining instructions.

FLAGs: none over 800 kcal. Label lines: yogurt (gelatine), chocolate (jaffa variation).
