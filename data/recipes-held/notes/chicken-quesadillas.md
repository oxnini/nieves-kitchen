# chicken-quesadillas (from draft m-quesadillas)

## Sources
- **RecipeTin Eats, Quesadilla!!! (stovetop) and Oven Baked Chicken Quesadillas.** Spice rub ratios for the chicken (cumin, paprika, garlic/onion powder, oregano, cayenne), sear chicken then rest before dicing, cool the filling so it does not steam the tortilla (the key anti-soggy rule), cheese on both sides as glue, medium-low/medium heat with a lid on the first side to melt the middle then uncovered to crisp, flip over the folded edge, and the oven batch method (hot oiled tray, 220°C, 8 min, flip, about 8 min) used in variations. Her filling also includes corn, which fits the cook's note.
- **Isabel Eats, Chicken Quesadillas** (read via Wayback). The splash of soy sauce on the chicken for depth (kept, halal-certified), seasoned whole pieces seared then rested and chopped small, onion and pepper cooked in the same pan, leftover or rotisserie chicken as a shortcut, corn and black beans as add-ins.
- **Rick Bayless (rickbayless.com, Gringas al Pastor / grilled shrimp quesadillas, via search summaries).** Flour tortilla quesadillas on a griddle about 2 min a side, oil or butter brushed on the outside for colour; flip once the bottom is browned and cheese is melting. Used for the brush-before-flip step.
- **America's Test Kitchen article "this quesadilla has cheese on the inside and outside"** and **Tasting Table / The Takeout pieces on the cheese crust** (via search summaries). The skirt technique: cheese scattered straight into a preheated non-stick pan, tortilla laid on top, medium heat (hot enough to crisp, low enough not to burn), non-stick essential or the cheese glues to the pan; the idea comes from Mexico City taco stands putting cheese on the griddle first (costra). Became the whole "cheese skirt" section and the equipment line.
- **Pati Jinich, Tinga Dillas** (via search summary): medium-low, until the cheese is fully melted and the tortillas hardened on both sides; Monterey Jack as the melting cheese (in substitutions).

## Picks applied
- Fork method: no pick, so the drafted default "pan, one at a time" is the method. The un-picked oven batch is in variations.
- Tweak skirt (ticked): its own section, "The cheese skirt", with 80g of the cheese set aside (20g per quesadilla), medium heat, scatter, wait 30 s, lay the quesadilla on, lid 3 min, uncovered 1 to 2 min until lacy and quiet, lift test. Equipment and tips support it.
- Cook's note "I like to add corn if I have it": optional 150g sweetcorn in the filling list, with its own step: tinned drained and patted dry, or frozen straight in, charred in a dry non-stick pan over high heat 5 to 6 min (a couple more for frozen) until blistered. Mentioned in the description and headnote as the cook's habit.
- "Make the chicken juicy and well seasoned" (brief): thighs, 15 min to 1 day in salt, spices, oil and soy; seared undisturbed; 75°C; rested 5 min; chopped small and returned with all the board juices; lime, fresh chili, coriander; taste and adjust.
- Draft extras: sour cream dropped (cook does not use it); salsa and guacamole kept as serve-alongside; draft's mozzarella and mature cheddar mix kept; oil or butter for the outside kept (brushed on before the flip). Un-ticked tweaks pickled jalapeños, black beans and corn (beans part), and chipotle chicken are in variations.

## Decisions I made for the cook
- Raw chicken thighs (500g) as the main method; the draft's cooked/shredded chicken became the shortcut substitution, and breast is a substitution with butterflying advice.
- 2 tsp halal-certified soy in the rub (Isabel Eats): real depth for little effort, and the cook already keeps halal soy for other recipes; "leave it out" in substitutions.
- Fresh green chili raw in the filling (cook's taste), plus garlic cloves with the peppers on top of the garlic powder.
- One large 25cm tortilla folded per person (4 quesadillas, serves 4), 50g cheese inside each (25g each side) plus 20g for the skirt: 280g cheese total.
- Skirt on the first side only; the second side is brushed with oil or butter and pressed. A skirt both sides would add ~90 kcal a serving and is fiddly to flip.
- Lid for the first 3 minutes so a just-warm filling heats through, then uncovered so the skirt dries and crisps (as in the house grilled-cheese method).
- Same pan for corn, chicken and vegetables (one pan to wash), wiped clean before the quesadillas.
- Coordinates Mexico City; influences Mexico then United States of America; featuredIngredients ['chicken'] (the title ingredient).
- Tags high-protein, comfort food, meal-prep friendly (filling keeps 2 days; uncooked quesadillas freeze). Not "quick" at 70 min total.

## Nutrition arithmetic (whole recipe, then /4)
- Chicken thighs skinless 500g raw at ~160 kcal / 19g P / 9g F per 100g = 800 kcal, 95 P, 45 F.
- Oil for chicken 1 tbsp = 120 kcal, 14 F. Soy 2 tsp = 6 kcal, 1 P. Spices ~4 tsp = 25 kcal, 1 P, 3 C, 1 F.
- Onion ~150g = 60 kcal, 13 C, 1.5 P. Red pepper ~150g = 45 kcal, 9 C, 1.5 P. Garlic 2 cloves = 8 kcal, 2 C. Lime juice 1 tbsp = 5 kcal, 1 C.
- Sweetcorn 150g (counted, since the cook usually adds it) = 120 kcal, 4 P, 22 C, 1.5 F.
- 4 large flour tortillas ~62g (~190 kcal, 32 C, 5 P, 4.5 F each) = 760 kcal, 128 C, 20 P, 18 F.
- Cheese 280g: 140g low-moisture mozzarella (~304 kcal, 24.7 P, 22.5 F per 100g = 425 kcal, 34.6 P, 31.5 F) + 140g mature cheddar (416 kcal, 25 P, 34.9 F per 100g = 582 kcal, 35 P, 48.9 F) = 1007 kcal, 69.6 P, 80.4 F, ~3 C.
- Brushing oil 2 tsp = 80 kcal, 9 F.
- Totals: ~3036 kcal, ~193 P, ~181 C, ~169 F. Per serving: **760 kcal, 48 P, 45 C, 42 F** (check: 48x4 + 45x4 + 42x9 = 750; difference is rounding and fibre).
- Excludes the serve-alongside extras: salsa adds ~10 kcal a serving, shop guacamole ~50 kcal (which would bring it to ~810). Without corn it is ~730.

## Verification

Cross-checked the cheese skirt against Tasting Table (crispy cheese quesadilla: non-stick or griddle, a film of oil or butter so the cheese sticks to the tortilla not the pan, medium heat once the cheese is in, flip when the cheese is browned and bubbly at the edge and lifts off easily) and a search round-up of crispy-cheese quesadilla recipes (medium heat, non-stick or the cheese glues to the pan, let the leaked cheese brown 2 to 3 min before flipping). The recipe's medium heat, 20g per skirt, lid for 3 min then uncovered until lacy and quiet, then flip, matches; the lid is what lets the 50g of inside cheese melt before the tortilla colours. Chicken (thighs, 0.9% salt plus 2 tsp soy, seared then rested, 75°C, juices returned) is sound; salt across the dish is about 3.5g a serving, sane. Corn handling (drain, pat dry, char dry so it adds no water to the filling) is right. Ingredients and steps reconcile (cheese 4 x 25g x 2 + 4 x 20g = 280g).

Changes:
1. `time` 50/70 to 70/90: corn char, 10 to 11 min of searing, 6 min of vegetables, 10 min cooling, filling, then four quesadillas at about 8 min each in one pan add up to about 90 min start to last plate. Headnote now says about 8 min each and "a good half hour"; dropped "two pans halve that", since the skirt wants the non-stick pan.
2. Corn moved into the chicken's 15-minute sit (prep step now says char it then), so the timing works.
3. Corn char: no longer "heat a non-stick pan over high heat with no oil" open-ended; pan heats empty for 1 minute only, then the corn goes in (empty non-stick on high is bad for the coating).
4. Chicken sear cue changed from "deep golden" (a spice-rubbed thigh never looks golden) to "a deep, spiced brown", with "turn down to medium if the spices blacken".
5. Cast iron: equipment no longer says non-stick is essential; a well-seasoned cast-iron pan with a few drops of oil works if you wait for the skirt to crisp. Skirt step and the brushing-oil ingredient name carry the "few drops for cast iron" line.
6. Skirt placement: a 25cm folded tortilla does not fit the 22cm flat base of a 28cm pan, so the half-moon of cheese now sits with its straight side off-centre and most of the cheese along the curved edge where the frill shows; the quesadilla slides on off a board or wide spatula, fold along the straight side, and the tips riding up is explained.
7. Added an oven-preheat step (120°C (100°C fan), rack over a tray) at the start of the skirt section, so the keep-warm oven is hot when the first one comes off.
8. Added a heat note for successive quesadillas: a heavy pan keeps building heat, so turn down a notch if the skirt goes deep golden before 4 minutes.
9. Freezer note: cook from frozen "until the middle is piping hot" (cooked chicken reheated inside).
10. Nutrition left at 760/48/45/42: my re-estimate lands within a few per cent.

FLAG (kept): cheese rennet label check; halal-certified soy. 760 kcal, under 800 (about 810 with a generous shop guacamole on the side).
