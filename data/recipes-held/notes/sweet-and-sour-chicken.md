## What makes it not bog-standard
1. **Two-layer coating left to go damp** (RecipeTin double dredge, Pups with Chopsticks rest, house crispy beef). 2 tbsp of cornflour stirred into the egg-white chicken makes a sticky glue layer, then the dry dredge, then 10 minutes on the tray until the coat turns tacky. It fries into one hard shell instead of flaking off and leaving white specks, and the 10 minutes is when the oil is heating anyway.
2. **A real double fry with the rest built in** (ATK, Made With Lau, RecipeTin). 4 min at 170°C in three batches, then 1 to 2 min at 190°C. The time it takes the oil to climb to 190°C is the rest that lets moisture move out to the crust. Doneness is checked by cutting a piece open (74°C).
3. **Vegetables flash-fried for 1 minute and taken out before the sauce** (Made With Lau). They go back in only for the off-heat toss, so the peppers and onion never simmer and stay crunchy. Pineapple gets 30 seconds in the sauce just to warm it.
4. **Thick sauce, heat off, 15-second toss** (RecipeTin path test, Made With Lau heat-off, ATK). The sauce has to turn glossy and hold a spatula trail before anything goes in, so it coats the crust and does not soak it.
5. **A last tablespoon of rice vinegar stirred in off the heat** (supported by the Shandong sweet and sour fish method, where the vinegar goes in after the sauce is thickened). Boiling drives off vinegar's sharp aroma, and this puts it back, which is the "bright, not cloying" brief. Together with juice-not-syrup pineapple, plain rice vinegar and white rather than brown sugar, it keeps the sauce sharp.

## Decisions I made for the cook
- **Serves 4** (the draft said 3 to 4). 150g of raw thigh each with lots of vegetables and rice is a normal portion, and it keeps the dish at 530 kcal.
- **Coating cornflour raised from 100g to 120g.** 100g was tight once 2 tbsp go into the glue layer; it is marked "you will have some left over". The nutrition counts only what sticks.
- **Rice vinegar 4 tbsp (3 in the sauce + 1 to finish) instead of 3**, and **pineapple juice 150ml instead of 120ml**, which is about what a 432g tin gives. This makes it brighter and gives enough sauce for 600g chicken plus about 650g of vegetables and fruit.
- **Cornflour in the sauce raised from 1 tbsp to 4 tsp**, whisked into the jug rather than added as a separate slurry. That matches RecipeTin's thickness for the same volume of sauce, and one jug is more foolproof.
- **Added 10g ginger** with the garlic (the cook's habit; Takes Two Eggs, Tasting Table and RecipeTin all use it).
- **Garnish**: spring onions and toasted sesame seeds, with green chili and coriander as an optional pile-on (the cook's habit).
- **Second pan for the sauce**, as in the house crispy chili beef, so no hot oil ever has to move. There is a one-wok fallback with the 10-minute cool-down.
- `featuredIngredients: ['chicken', 'vinegar']`. Vinegar is the "sour" and defines the dish, and the pantry note ("a splash at the end wakes up...") matches the finishing move. Drop `vinegar` if you think only proteins should feature here; no other recipe features it yet.
- Coordinates are Hong Kong (22.32, 114.17), checked with d3-geo to sit inside the China polygon. `influences: ['China']` only.
- Tags: high-protein (32g, threshold 25), comfort food, treat (deep fried). Difficulty Medium (two fries plus hot oil), like crispy chili beef.
- `isGlutenFree: false` because of the soy (the tamari swap is in substitutions). `isDairyFree: true`.
- Time: 50 active, 60 total (15 min marinade with prep, 5 to coat, 10 rest while the oil heats, about 24 min frying, 4 min sauce).
